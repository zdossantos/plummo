## Interfaces

TribunalGame owns preparing/speaking_first/speaking_second/voting/reveal/waiting and JSON queue/tour/topic/votes. Engine owns paused/resuming/results and room locking. POST tribunal/{finish,vote} includes game_id and round; vote choice is 0 (for) or 1 (against). View exposes advocate IDs and current speaker, votes only at verdict; personal canSpeak/canVote/answered/points.
